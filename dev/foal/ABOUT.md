# Foals in HorseDynamics

Foals for Farming Simulator 25: a baked, animated foal model for every horse coat, shop pictures, foals that are led and not ridden. The logic is `scripts/HorseFoal.lua` (xml/horseFoal.xml is the foal vehicle, xml/foalAnimals.xml the animal config); the foal itself is `models/foal/`.

## How we got to the model: `dev/foal/`

The workshop is part of this repo, with its history (`git log -- dev`): a browser app with sliders (`dev/foal/app`), the Blender scripts and the bake tools (`dev/foal/tools`). The recipe, in short:

1. `dev/foal/app` (foal-scale): shape the foal by sliders and presets (Favorite 1..6), look at it in the walk and idle poses beside the adult.
2. `dev/foal/tools/bake_foal.py`: turn that look into `horseFoal.i3d`, `.shapes` and `.anim` (bone scales, vertex edits, every clip converted); `check_bake.py` compares the result with the app.
3. `dev/foal/tools/blender_icon.py`: the shop pictures, one per coat.
4. Copy the result into `models/foal/` and `store/foal/`, then `bash build/deploy.sh`.

What is not in git (it comes from the game's own horse): `dev/foal/app/horse_app.json`, `dev/obj/`, `dev/out/`, `dev/foal/app/vendor/` (three.js). They are left out of git because they are the game's data; the foal model itself (`models/foal`) is in the repo with credit, see the README's Credits section.

The build (`build/build.sh`) packs only `store/ models/foal/ xml/ scripts/ ...` (HorseDynamics' build): `dev/foal/` never goes into the mod.

## Coat shader experiment (2026-11-12)

The foal's body is drawn by `shaders/foalCoatShader.xml`, a copy of the game's horse skin shader with our additions (variation `coatDirt` for the body; the mane keeps `dirtEnabled` and only gets the tint):

* `python3 dev/foal/tools/make_coat_mask.py <HorseModel dir> models/foal` makes `models/foal/coatMask.dds` from the game's mesh: R coat skin, G eyeballs, B hooves, A pattern A (the white patches of the game's Pinto tile). Check it by overlaying it on a tile (it lines up with the eye discs, hoof shapes and the Pinto patches).
* `python3 dev/foal/tools/make_coat_mask2.py <HorseModel dir> models/foal [--preview]` makes `models/foal/coatMask2.dds` (uncompressed, the gradients must not band): the markings as fields computed in 3D on the horse mesh, R spot field, G blanket gradient, B face levels (star, strip, snip), A sock gradient. `--preview` writes side and head views with a bay coat to the output folder. The shapes are our own sketches of the idea (blaze, socks, blanket with spots, snowflake flecks), not copies of any real horse.
* `python3 dev/foal/tools/make_coat_shader.py <game data dir>` writes the shader and points `models/foal/horseFoal.i3d` at it (variation, mask, parameters). Run it again after every bake (the bake writes the game's shader path back). `--revert` points the foal model at the game's own shader again.
* In the game, on a foal that has been led out of its pasture: `hdCoat tint 1.2 0.9 0.8` (colour of the coat only), `hdCoat shade 0.8 1.1` (brightness, saturation), `hdCoat gloss 0.8` (a sun glint and a smoother surface on the eyeballs; try 1 and look at the eye from the side with the sun behind you), `hdCoat pattern 1` (the Pinto patches in white), `hdCoat pattern 1 0.9 0.6 0.3` (in another colour), `hdCoat reset`, `all` at the end for every foal. If the foal looks pink, black or invisible the shader did not load: run `make_coat_shader.py --revert`, deploy, and read the log for the shader error.
* The markings, same console: `hdCoat face 2` (0 none, 1 star, 2 blaze, 3 blaze + snip; fractions grow it), `hdCoat socks 0.5`, `hdCoat blanket 0.45 1` (white rump, dark spots inside; 1 1 = leopard), `hdCoat flecks 1 0.1` (snowflake; the second number 0 big .. 0.8 tiny), `hdCoat white 0.9 0.85 0.7` (colour of all the white). Combine with `hdCoat tint` for the base colour: snowflake on a chestnut, blanket on a bay, leopard on a black.
* Spots are made in the shader (2026-11-12, the mask version thinned out at a distance): `hdCoat flecks 1` (white flecks), `hdCoat blanket 0.45 1` (dark spots in the blanket) and `hdCoat spots 0.7 0.8 0 0.08` (density, size, seed, cell size in metres: the look; another seed, another arrangement) come from the undeformed body position, so they stay on the skin and need no texture detail. Edges are never narrower than 3/4 of a pixel (ddx / ddy of the position): far away a spot turns soft and faint instead of vanishing or sparkling. The mask's R channel is the density map (`make_coat_mask2.py`: full on the body and quarters, neck less, head about a third, none on the muzzle, eyelids and ear tips, thin on the lower legs, mild patchiness), so no spot lands where none should be.

## The adult clone (2026-11-12)

The grown horses (pasture and ridden) use `models/horse/horseAdult.i3d`, our own copy of the game's horse, so the coat shader (spots, markings) can be tested on a horse that is ridden. It is baked like the foal, from the app with NO changes (`foal-scale.html?bake=1`: every setting 1, the overall size too: it comes out within 1 mm of the game's own horse, so the halter, the harness and the saddle fit it). The first version had a bigger head and a thicker neck so it could be told apart; the halter went through that head, so it was dropped. Recipe: `foal-scale.html?bake=1` through headless Edge, then `python3 dev/foal/tools/bake_foal.py bake.json <HorseModel dir> <out dir> horseAdult`, copy to `models/horse/`, `python3 dev/foal/tools/make_coat_shader.py <game data dir>` (patches both models; the clone reaches into `models/foal/` for the two masks, the UV layout is the same). `xml/horseAdult.xml` is the game's horse.xml with the model path changed. Switch off with `HorseFoal.ADULT_CLONE = false`. `hdCoat` now acts on the nearest foal or adult horse, the ridden one included. Gear (halter, harness) is still fitted to the game's adult head: a 15% bigger head may sit a little off.

## Random looks (2026-11-12, `scripts/HorseCoat.lua`)

Every horse gets a random look the moment it exists (dealer, birth) or is first shown (an older horse): a face marking (none 34 %, star, blaze, blaze and snip), socks (none 40 %, else a height 0.2 .. 0.8, each leg a little different) and a pattern (plain 46 %, snowflake, sparse flecks, blanket with spots, plain blanket, leopard, pinto patches; appaloosa is the snowflake / blanket / leopard family). Only the markings: the base coat colour is the game's own tile. One number per horse, the seed (`cluster.hdCoatSeed`, saved in the animal's own XML attribute `hdCoatSeed`, in the init streams, kept by clone), so it looks the same on its pasture, led, ridden, after a save, and as a foal and as an adult. `HorseCoat.fromSeed` makes the markings from it (pure, tested in `tests/horsecoat_test.lua`). Shown on the vehicle when it gets its cluster (`Rideable.setCluster`) and on a pasture when it rebuilds its animals (`AnimalClusterHusbandry:updateVisuals`). Console: `hdCoat randomize [seed]` rolls the nearest horse vehicle again, `hdCoat seed` says what it has (and `hdCoat flecks 0` and the others tune it from there). Not visible in the animal dealer: it shows a picture per coat, not the model.

The look is also told on the information screens: a "Coat" line in the info box of a horse (look at it, on a pasture or a vehicle), and under the description of one of your own animals in the animal screen. The dealer's new animals are not clusters yet, so a purchase stays a surprise.

## Separate tack (2026-11-12, `split_tack.py`)

The game's saddle shape is ONE skinned mesh holding the saddle and the bridle. `python3 dev/foal/tools/split_tack.py models/horse/horseAdult` (and `models/foal/horseFoal`) splits it into a saddle and a bridle shape: the mesh is 15 connected pieces, the head group (the big bridle, the noseband, the bit rings: 3915 vertices) is the bridle, the seat, stirrups and leathers (2487) the saddle; the group is decided on the game's own mesh and applied by vertex number, so every baked model splits the same way. Weights and bone indices are untouched (both shapes use the saddle node's `skinBindNodeIds`); in the i3d the bridle is the saddle node's CHILD, visible exactly when the saddle is (nothing changes by default) and `hdTack bridle off` hides it alone (`hdTack show` first: the game shows the tack only when ridden). Run it after every bake, after `make_coat_shader.py`. Needs the skinned writer of `tools/make_hitching_post.shape_entity(skin=(weights, indices))`: option bit 0x40, then 4 float weights and 4 byte bone indices per vertex after the uvs. The tack room vision (pieces as siblings, shown by script): memory project-fs25-tack-room-vision.

Attach nodes (2026-11-12, `add_attach_nodes.py`, run after `split_tack.py`): `bridleRing`, a child of Head_M at the bridle's bit ring, worked out from each model's own bridle geometry (the adult comes out 1.6 mm from HorseRope.ring; the foal at (-0.067 0.241 0.040), where the script's adult offset put its rope about 20 cm beyond the real ring). `HorseRope.ringAnchor` uses the baked node when the model has it. The next ones: the lead ring of the halter, `traceL` / `traceR` at the harness tug rings, `hitchRear` at the rump hook (memory project-fs25-tack-room-vision).

## The distance bug: ImageMagick's mip levels (2026-11-12)

Spots, face markings, socks, even the tint, vanished at a few metres, one "sweet spot", and came back, weaker, further away; it looked like TAA, minimum sizes and LODs and was none of them. The mip levels ImageMagick wrote into `coatMask.dds` and `coatMask2.dds` were broken: it resizes an RGBA image weighting the colour channels by the ALPHA channel, which is 0 over most of the body (the sock field, the pinto pattern), so at the first smaller level the spot density fell from 0.478 to 0.040, the blanket 0.238 -> 0.025, the face field to 0 and the coat mask R (a factor of the tint and of every marking) 0.591 -> 0.170, climbing back at coarser levels (0.72 at 16 px). The first smaller level is reached at about 5 m on an adult, 3 m on a foal. Fix: `mask_mips.py` makes the chain ourselves, a plain 2 x 2 average per channel (all channels plain averages: the face and sock fields were MAX pooled for a while and a leg island's sock values leaked over the UV border as a white line along the topline); `make_coat_mask.py` recompresses each level to DXT5 with it, `make_coat_mask2.py` writes the raw levels. Check a mask file by decoding its levels: the channel means must stay about the same at every level. The distance helpers in the shader (sharper mask reading, minimum spot size, 45 % floor) were all built on the wrong diagnosis: `hdCoat raw` shows the shader without them, and should now be fine; they can probably be turned down.

Follow-up (same day): with `hdCoat raw` on, a thin white line along the horse's topline was gone: the shader read the mask three mip levels sharper than the screen asks (a trick against the broken mips) and that drew the line next to the UV border. Removed: the mask is read plainly now. What stays of the helpers, all tunable and all off with `hdCoat raw`: the least size of a spot on the screen (default 2 px radius) and the 45 % floor of spots kept when they are enlarged.

Fur in the white (2026-11-12): the markings and the pinto patches replaced the coat colour with one flat white, which threw away the fur of the base texture and looked like spray paint. Now the base texel's brightness against the coarse average round it (`baseMap` sampled at lod 4, 85 % of the ratio, clamped 0.55 .. 1.5) multiplies the white, so the fur structure and shading of the coat underneath show through the markings. It sits in `#if defined( COAT_MASK )` (the body only). If a horse ever shows pink or not at all after this, the log has a `foalCoatShader.xml(...)` compile error: remove the `furDetail` lines in `dev/foal/tools/make_coat_shader.py`.

Fur in the white is optional (the user does not like it): `markWhite.a` / `hdCoat fur 0..1`, default 0 = flat white.

## Plain coats (no native white)
The game's coat atlas has native white (a blaze strip on the face, white socks) on tiles (2,0) and (2,1). `dev/foal/tools/make_plain_coats.py` paints it out into the local body colour (fur detail kept), writes `dev/foal/horseCoats_diffuse.png` (git-ignored), and `paintedWhite.png`; the PNG goes through the GIANTS textureTool (BC1) to `models/foal/horseCoats_diffuse.dds`, which both horse i3d files use instead of `horse_diffuse`. `make_coat_mask.py` adds the painted texels to the coat skin (R) so the tint and our markings reach them. The pinto tile is untouched. Dealer store pictures still show the old markings.

Tack pieces (2026-11-12, `split_tack.py`, run after every bake): the game's one saddle mesh is three shapes under the `saddle` group (the game's equipmentNode, shown/hidden together as before): `bridle` (the head group), `saddleSeat` (seat, panels, stirrups, girth) and `blanket` (the saddle's biggest UV island, a rounded sheet draped over the back, 430 vertices). `hdTack <name> on|off` hides any one alone. New shapes are appended as the group's last children, so the mapping paths do not shift.

Halter and blanket fill (2026-11-12): `split_tack.py` also cuts `bit` (bit, rings, strap below the noseband) and `cheek` (strap down to the noseband) out of the bridle shape. `add_halter.py` draws a skinned `halter` shape (one clean cheek strap per side along the cheek's centre line, a chin ring under the noseband's lowest point; hidden in the model) and the node `halterRing` under Head_M that the lead rope ties to in halter mode (`HorseRope.targetNodeOf`). `fill_blanket.py` covers the blanket's opening (the game's pad is only a frame) with a surface that follows the back (body mesh heights + the frame's clearance) and a plain patch of the pad fabric. Run in this order after a bake: make_coat_shader, split_tack, add_attach_nodes, add_halter, fill_blanket. `hdTack halter` = bridle without bit.

Blanket, rebuilt (2026-11-12, `rebuild_blanket.py`, replaces what fill_blanket.py patched; run after it): the pad is ONE sheet made of the body mesh's own triangles under the game's pad (subdivided once), lifted 1 cm along the body normals, then low-pass smoothed (Taubin, 40 iterations) and kept at least 6 mm off the body, so its top line is even over the horse's bumps (that is the point of a pad). One boundary loop, no holes (checked). The 46 triangles of the frame that are the saddle's girth billets went back into the saddle shape. Fabric: a plain patch of the pad texture for now. The old pad is kept in `dev/foal/blanket_old/<model>.npz`, later runs start from it.

Stirrups (2026-11-13): the game's saddle shape holds the stirrup iron (a 54 x 172 x 143 mm plate of chrome and leather loops, skinned to the leaf bones jnts_forLeg_L/R) and the stirrup leather (the thin 3 cm strap from y 1.10 to 1.58). The riding pose is the game's own. `stirrup_bones.py` (called by rebuild_blanket.py) adds a bone `stirrupJoint_L/R` under Spine1_M at the top of each leather (last child, node ids 113 / 114, appended to the saddleSeat shape's skinBindNodeIds) and puts the iron and the lower leather 100 % on it, fading to the old bones at the leather's top. `HorseStirrup.lua` turns the bone about the horse's lateral axis (setQuaternion, axis from localDirectionToLocal): `SWING_DEG` (13, forward) while nobody rides, 0 while somebody does; `hdStirrup <deg>` tunes it. An earlier girth-details pass mistook the iron for roller buckles and the leather for a billet and moved them 13 cm forward: that is gone; the stirrups are exempt from the pad's weight sharing.
